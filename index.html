Research

Latency. Stress.
Reactivation.

After the first infection, HSV‑1 never leaves. It stays silent inside neurons for life. His work explains what happens inside the cell at the moment the virus decides to return.

Phase 0 · Latency
Phase 0

Latency.

The viral genome sits silenced inside the neuron’s nucleus. No replication, and nothing for the immune system to see. It can stay this way for decades.

Phase I

Stress signal.

Neuronal stress switches on pathways such as c‑Jun. UL12.5 makes mitochondria release nucleic acids, and the cell’s innate alarm goes off.

Phase II

Reactivation.

Instead of stopping the virus, the alarm helps it wake. Without UL12.5, reactivation fails. Switching the same pathways on directly restores it. New virions travel down the axon.
